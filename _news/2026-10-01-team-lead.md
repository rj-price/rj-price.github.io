I've started a new role as Bioinformatics Team Lead at Niab. The team works on fungal and plant genomics and builds computational tools for research groups across the institute. Current projects include dsRNA design for RNAi-based control of _Fusarium_ and _Phytophthora_ on the Innovate UK mycoDOT project, assembling tetraploid honeyberry genomes, and a pan-genome survey of NLR resistance genes in common bean.
