Our _Fusarium oxysporum_ f. sp. _lactucae_ resistance-breaking paper is now on [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.30.755575v1). We sequenced ten isolates, including the first Fola2 and Fola3 genomes and a putative new race from France (FolaX), and found that lettuce pathogenicity arose independently in several _F. oxysporum_ lineages, which then shared effectors through horizontal transfer.
